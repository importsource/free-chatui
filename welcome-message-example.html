✨ Best Practices for Welcome Messages

Do's:

  • ✅ Keep it short and friendly (under 60 characters is ideal)
  • ✅ Use emojis to add warmth and personality
  • ✅ Set expectations (tell users what you can help with)
  • ✅ Use action-oriented language ("Ask me," "Let me help")
  • ✅ Match your brand voice (casual vs. professional)
  • ✅ Test on mobile to ensure it displays well

Don'ts:

  • ❌ Don't use overly formal or robotic language
  • ❌ Don't make it too long (overwhelming)
  • ❌ Don't use jargon or technical terms
  • ❌ Don't forget to proofread for typos
  • ❌ Don't skip the welcome message (first impressions matter!)
