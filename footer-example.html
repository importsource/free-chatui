📄 Chat with Custom Footer

This example demonstrates the new customizable footer feature:

  • Fully Customizable: Add any HTML content to the footer
  • Copyright Info: Display copyright, legal notices, or terms
  • Links: Add links to privacy policy, terms of service, etc.
  • Custom Styling: Control background, color, font size, padding, and border
  • Responsive: Works seamlessly on all devices
Try it: Click the chat button to see the custom footer at the bottom of the chat window with copyright information and a link! Also notice the scroll-to-bottom button that appears when you scroll up in the messages.
Example Configuration: 
footer: {
  enabled: true,
  html: '© 2024 Your Company | Privacy Policy',
  background: '#f9fafb',
  color: '#6b7280',
  fontSize: '12px',
  padding: '12px 16px',
  borderTop: '1px solid #e5e7eb'
}
More Examples:

Simple Copyright:
html: '© 2024 Your Company. All rights reserved.'

With Links:
html: 'Powered by AI | Terms | Privacy'

Multi-line:
html: '© 2024 Company<br>Email: support@example.com'
