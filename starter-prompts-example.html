✨ Best Practices for Starter Prompts

Do's:

  • ✅ Keep prompts short and clear (under 50 characters)
  • ✅ Use 3-5 prompts (not too many to overwhelm)
  • ✅ Address the most common questions first
  • ✅ Use action verbs when appropriate
  • ✅ Add emojis for visual appeal (optional)
  • ✅ Test each prompt to ensure it works

Don'ts:

  • ❌ Don't use more than 6 prompts (keeps it clean)
  • ❌ Don't make prompts too long
  • ❌ Don't use technical jargon
  • ❌ Don't duplicate similar prompts
  • ❌ Don't forget to test on mobile

How They Work

  • Display: Prompts appear after the welcome message
  • Click to send: Users click a prompt to send it instantly
  • Stay visible: Prompts remain available throughout the conversation
  • Reusable: Users can click multiple prompts as needed
  • Persist: Prompts stay visible until conversation is reset
  • Smooth animation: Fade in with slide effect

Recommended Prompt Count

  • 2-3 prompts: Minimal, clean, perfect for mobile
  • 4 prompts: Balanced, recommended for most use cases
  • 5-6 prompts: Maximum before it feels crowded
