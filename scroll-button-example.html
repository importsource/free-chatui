📜 Scroll to Bottom Button

This example demonstrates the new scroll-to-bottom button feature:

  • Auto-Appear: Button appears when you scroll up from the bottom
  • Smart Detection: Shows when you're more than 100px from the bottom
  • Smooth Scroll: Click to smoothly scroll back to the latest messages
  • Auto-Hide: Disappears when you're at the bottom
  • Elegant Design: Circular button with icon in the bottom-right corner
Try it:
  1. Click the chat button to open the chat
  2. Click the "Add 10 Messages" button below to simulate a long conversation
  3. Scroll up in the chat messages
  4. Notice the scroll-to-bottom button appears in the bottom-right
  5. Click it to smoothly scroll back to the latest message!
Features:
• Button only appears when scrolled up more than 100px from bottom
• Smooth scrolling animation when clicked
• Matches your theme's gradient colors
• Responsive and works on all devices
• Automatically hidden when at the bottom
